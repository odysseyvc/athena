Chapter 10

What is built

Every part of the system with its present state: what runs every day, what has run once, what is written and never run, and what does not exist.

This chapter is a table. It is the place to check any claim made elsewhere on the site. "Runs" means it is running on the owner's machine now. "Run once" means it was run for real at least once and the result is reported in the chapter named. "Tests only" means its rules are covered by automated tests with a pretend Windows or a pretend model, and it has not been seen working on the real thing. As of 23 November 2026, with 141 tests passing.
