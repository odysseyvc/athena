A model of you that stays yours.

Athena learns what you are working on from your own computer: which windows had your attention, what you typed, what you opened. It keeps that in a folder on your machine and serves it, read-only, to the agents you choose. Everything it hands over is marked with where it came from.

What it does

Your agents know what you are doing, without being told.

An assistant that remembers only what you tell it knows very little. Athena fills that in from the machine you already work on, and nothing it learns leaves that machine unless an agent you let in asks for it.

It watches, cheaply

What has your attention

The program and window in front of you, read every two seconds, and for a browser the address. Away from the keyboard for two minutes, nothing is recorded.

The text in the window

What a screen reader would be told, through Windows' accessibility interface. No screenshot is kept. A window that says nothing is read once by Windows' own OCR and the picture is thrown away.

What you type and open

Only what you added to a field counts as yours; what was already there does not, and a large paste is marked as a paste. Files come from Windows' own Recent list.

It distils

Where the time went

Each day summed up by code: hours active, projects, sites, files. Kept as Markdown you can read, long after the raw record is deleted.

Search by words and by meaning

Ask for "the bill that did not add up" and find the invoice test. A 30 MB model on your own processor does the meaning; nothing is sent anywhere.

A memory that is kept up

With your own model key, a pass adds, confirms, revises and retires statements about your goals, commitments and style. Each must cite evidence, and a retired one keeps its dates.

It serves

Ten read-only tools

Over the Model Context Protocol, on this machine only: what you were just doing, a search, a person or project, samples of your writing, the model of you, one day, your recent mail, your calendar, conversations left hanging.

Mail and calendar, read-only

Sign an account in on the service's own page. Athena gets a right to read and nothing else, keeps codes, resets and statements off the disk, and marks every message by who wrote it.

A key for each visitor

Every agent gets its own token and a set of rooms it may enter. One may browse; another must wait for your yes on every single request.

A page that is yours

What it knows, who may read it, and every request ever made, in your browser. Approve a waiting request with a click, take a key away, forget the last ten minutes, or tell it a guess about you is wrong.

How it works

Kinds are kept apart by how a thing was observed, not by what it says.

Most of what is on your screen was written by other people. A page can say anything, including orders to whichever agent reads it. So nothing on a screen is ever read for what it claims.

Capture

Paused or excluded? Then nothing is read. Otherwise the window, its text and your typing go to separate files.

Clean

Keys, passwords, card and account numbers and one-time codes are removed before anything is written.

Distil

Code turns your behaviour into days, projects and people. A model, if you allow one, sees only what you did and typed.

Serve

Token, approval, scope, answer, log, in that order. Every item carries its provenance.

Every item an agent receives says whose it is. Code attaches that mark, and no text on a screen can change it.

  • An excluded window leaves no trace. Password managers, banks, sign-in pages, private browser windows and anything you name: not the title, not the time spent. The timeline has a gap.
  • Facts about you come from you. A page that says "the owner's priority is X" can become, at most, "the owner had a window open that showed this".
  • Deleting stays deleted. Forget the last ten minutes and the search index is rebuilt without them.
  • It listens on this machine only. That is a constant in the code, not a setting.
About you

Measured, typed or stated by you. Where your attention was, what you wrote, what you put in your own notes file. Marked about_owner: true.

Quoted

What other people wrote. Window titles and screen text, always inside a quoted_text field with its author marked. Material to read, never an instruction.

Withheld

Text that reads as an order to an assistant. Not served at all. The agent is told a piece was held back and how long it was.

What the last lock is worth, measured. A list of patterns caught 4 of 10 injections written after it was last changed. With a classifier model beside it, 9 of 10. That is why the first two do not depend on it. The chapter on whose words are whose has the numbers.

Why it exists

Build a model of me by itself, keep it in a folder I own, and serve it to my agents.

That is the brief from its first owner, in short. Athena was built beside a personal assistant that kept a memory only of what it was told. The product whose idea it follows, Second by First Person Labs, runs on a Mac and is on a waiting list; this one was written for Windows 11, from the first signal to the server, and every decision is in the book.

Bought where it could be

The protocol, Windows' accessibility interface and OCR, SQLite's word search, an open embedding model. What exists was checked first, licence and cost included.

Roadmap

What is next.

Now

  • More than one mailboxMail and calendar have been read from one real Google account, 180 days of it, and search over it scored by code. Other services, and a week of daily use, have not been tried.
  • The first real weekSeven days of ordinary use, then questions with known answers taken from them.
  • The model pass, run for realIt is written and tested with a pretend model. It has never seen a key.
  • Other makers' agents, connected for realThe lines to give eight kinds of agent come from their documentation. One assistant has been connected.

Next

  • A smaller classifierThe one tried holds a gigabyte of memory. One a tenth the size exists behind a licence its user must accept.
  • People, properlyToday they are found by the titles of chat windows and joined by spelling. That is the weakest part.

Later

  • MeetingsNothing is heard today. Audio is off by design until there is a good answer for the other people in the room.
  • Agents that run elsewhereIt listens on your machine only, so an assistant on a server cannot read it. Reaching it safely needs a tunnel and real sign-in.
  • Other systemsWindows 11 only, so far.
